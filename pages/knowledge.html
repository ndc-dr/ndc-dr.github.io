Authoritative technical specifications, operational topologies, maintenance criteria, and failure protection models for NDC facility engineers.

1. Substation Step-Down: Cast Resin Dry-Type Transformers (2000 kVA & 1600 kVA)

The intake power system utilizes two high-efficiency cast resin dry-type transformers stepping down the utility supply from 11 kV to 415 V 3-phase 50 Hz. Unlike oil-immersed transformers, cast resin dry-type units utilize epoxy resin under vacuum casting, eliminating dielectric oil spill hazards, environmental contamination, and toxic gas release risks inside enclosed data center plant rooms.

11 kV Medium Voltage Grid ➔ 3 sets of Single Phase Step Voltage Regulator - AVR ➔ 11 kV VCB Vacuum Switchgear ➔ Cast Resin Transformers (2000 kVA / 1600 kVA) ➔ Low Voltage (LV) Intake Bus
Transformer ArrayTransformer-1: 2000 kVA | Transformer-2: 1600 kVA
Insulation ClassClass F (Winding temperature rise 100K limit)
Cooling DesignationAN / AF (Air Natural convection with forced cooling fan cross-blow)
Fire Behavior RatingF1 (Low fire hazard, self-extinguishing epoxy, zero halogen emission)
Environmental / ClimaticE2 (Resistance to condensation/humidity) / C2 (Operating down to -25°C)
Thermal MonitoringPt100 RTD sensor embedded in core & LV windings with digital controller
Operating Rule: If winding temperature exceeds 130°C, the PT100 controller initiates automatic forced cooling fan blowers. If temperature reaches 150°C, a high-temperature alarm triggers on the BMS. Instant mechanical lockout trip occurs at 155°C.

2. Power Quality Regulation: 3600 kVA Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR)

The incoming MV(11KV) grid supply passes through a high-capacity **3600 kVA Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR)** prior to the transformation operation. The AVR neutralizes utility sags, brownouts, sustained overvoltages, and dynamic phase unbalances, isolating critical UPS rectifiers and mechanical chillers from severe utility grid variations.

Output Voltage Precision32 steps of correction
Efficiency≥ 98% at rated full capacity load
Cooling MethodONAN
Bypass IntegrationManual maintenance bypass and automatic static electronic bypass

3. Emergency Prime Generation: 3× 2000 kVA Weichai DCC-Rated Gensets

The emergency generation plant comprises **three 2000 kVA (1600 kWe each) Weichai diesel generator sets** rated under the **Data Center Continuous (DCC)** standard according to ISO 8528-1. DCC certification ensures these engines can operate without runtime limitations at 100% continuous data center load during sustained utility blackouts.

DG 1 (2000 kVA DCC) + DG 2 (2000 kVA DCC) + DG 3 (2000 kVA DCC) ➔ DG Control Panel(s)
Array Configuration3× 2000 kVA DCC / 415 V / 50 Hz / 1500 RPM
Redundancy SchemeN+1 Redundant Array (2 Sets Carry Total Peak Load + 1 Active Standby)
Step Load Capability100% G3 transient response complying with ISO 8528-5
Autonomous RuntimeContinuous prime operation supported by bulk fuel storage and day-tanks
Synchronizing TimeMains failure start signal: Synchronized & on-bus within 15 seconds

4. Low-Loss Distribution: Powerduct Pro BBT & NMW 2.1 Smart Busway

Power distribution replaces traditional high-cable bulk trays with an integrated busway backbone:

  • Powerduct Pro Series Busbar Trunking (BBT): Heavy-capacity, sandwich-structured, low-impedance busbar system interconnecting the Substation LV panels, AVR, Weichai Generator parallel switchboards, and the Huawei UPS inputs. The all-aluminum housing delivers 50% lighter dead-load with magnetic shielding and IP55/IP66 ingress protection.
  • NMW 2.1 Smart Busbar System: Overhead plug-in track busway deployed along the white space server corridors. It supports hot-swappable Tap-Off Boxes (TOBs) with embedded digital branch circuit meters, allowing engineers to connect or reconfigure power whips to rack PDUs without shutting down busway sections.
Key Maintenance Advantage: NMW 2.1 plug-in tap-off boxes allow live additions of server circuits with zero load disruption, adhering to Tier III Concurrent Maintainability requirements.

6. Energy Storage: Huawei SmartLi 3.0 Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) Battery

DC energy storage is driven by **Huawei SmartLi 3.0** battery energy storage cabinets using Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 / LFP) cell chemistry. LFP chemistry offers chemical stability with thermal runaway resistance, eliminating the thermal hazards typical of traditional lithium-cobalt or lead-acid batteries.

Chemistry & LifespanLithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4); up to 5,000 deep discharge cycles
Active Balance ControlActive current-balance control supports mixing new and old battery cabinets
Pack-Level Fire SuppressionBuilt-in localized fire extinguisher at module level for targeted suppression
Three-Level BMS ArchitectureCell Level ➔ Module Level ➔ Cabinet Level with cloud intelligence
Footprint AdvantageReduces floor load and footprint by up to 60–70% compared to equivalent VRLA banks
Safe Operation: SmartLi 3.0 monitors individual cell voltage, internal resistance, and terminal temperature every 5 minutes. If cell over-temperature occurs (> 60°C), the local BMS disconnects the string DC breaker and triggers localized aerosol suppression without affecting parallel strings.

12. High-Sensitivity Protection: Aspirating Smoke Detection System (VESDA)

White space fire safety uses **VESDA Aspirating Very Early Smoke Detection Apparatus**. High-efficiency aspirator pumps continuously draw air samples through networked piping installed directly at server rack exhaust grilles and cold aisle containment intake returns.

Detection ThresholdUltra-wide sensitivity: 0.001% to 20% obscuration/meter (Laser nephelometer)
Multi-Level Alert ThresholdsAlert ➔ Action ➔ Fire-1 ➔ Fire-2 progressive response staging
Containment IntegrationAt "Action" stage, FusionModule 2000 magnetic roof panels drop open
Suppression InterlockDual-zone coincidence confirmation triggers clean agent gas discharge release
Routine Testing Protocol: Per NFPA 72 and NFPA 75, VESDA sampling pipe transit time must be verified semi-annually at the farthest sampling hole. The transit duration must remain under 60 seconds.
